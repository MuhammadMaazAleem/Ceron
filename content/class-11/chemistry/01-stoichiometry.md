### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Using Celsius instead of Kelvin in gas law calculations.
  ✓ **Correction:** Always convert temperature to Kelvin: T(K) = t(°C) + 273.15.
  *Reason: Gas laws are strictly defined on the absolute thermodynamic temperature scale.*
