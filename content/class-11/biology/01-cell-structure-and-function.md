### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing lytic cycle with lysogenic cycle in viral reproduction.
  ✓ **Correction:** Lytic causes host cell burst; lysogenic integrates viral genome into host chromosome without immediate lysis.
  *Reason: Examiners deduct full marks for cycle confusion.*
