### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Using scalar addition for vector components.
  ✓ **Correction:** Always resolve vectors into rectangular components (Rx = ΣAx, Ry = ΣAy) before finding resultant.
  *Reason: Vectors add geometrically, not algebraically.*
