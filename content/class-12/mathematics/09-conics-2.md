### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Forgetting constant of integration (+ C) in indefinite integrals.
  ✓ **Correction:** Always add + C for every indefinite integral: ∫ f(x) dx = F(x) + C.
  *Reason: Examiners deduct 1 mark on every question with missing + C.*
