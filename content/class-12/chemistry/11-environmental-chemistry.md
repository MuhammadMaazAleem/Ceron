### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Writing single-headed arrows instead of double-headed curved arrows for electron pair movement.
  ✓ **Correction:** Curved arrows must always start from the electron pair (lone pair or bond) and point directly to the electrophilic atom.
  *Reason: Arrow conventions are strictly penalized if drawn incorrectly.*
