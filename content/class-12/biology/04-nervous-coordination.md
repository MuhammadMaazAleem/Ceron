### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Writing that the inside of an axon is positively charged at rest.
  ✓ **Correction:** At rest, the interior of the neuron is negative (-70 mV) relative to the exterior due to 3 Na+ pumped out for every 2 K+ pumped in.
  *Reason: The Na+/K+ ATPase electrogenic pump creates a polarized resting state.*
