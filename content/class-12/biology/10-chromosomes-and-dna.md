### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Pairing Adenine with Cytosine in nucleic acid synthesis.
  ✓ **Correction:** Adenine pairs with Thymine via 2 hydrogen bonds (A=T); Guanine pairs with Cytosine via 3 hydrogen bonds (G≡C). In RNA, Uracil replaces Thymine.
  *Reason: Complementary base pairing is fundamental to replication fidelity.*
