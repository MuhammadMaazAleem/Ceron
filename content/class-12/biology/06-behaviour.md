### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing kinesis with taxis.
  ✓ **Correction:** Kinesis is non-directional movement where speed changes with intensity; taxis is directional orientation toward or away from a stimulus.
  *Reason: Directionality is the defining scientific distinction.*
