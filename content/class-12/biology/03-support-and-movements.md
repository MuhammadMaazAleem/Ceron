### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing actin and myosin filaments in sarcomere contraction.
  ✓ **Correction:** Thin actin filaments slide inward over thick myosin filaments during cross-bridge power strokes, shortening the sarcomere.
  *Reason: Myosin heads bind actin active sites exposed when Ca2+ binds troponin.*
