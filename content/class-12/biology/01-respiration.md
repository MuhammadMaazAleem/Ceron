### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing external respiration (pulmonary ventilation) with cellular respiration (mitochondrial ATP generation).
  ✓ **Correction:** External respiration is physical gas exchange in lungs; cellular respiration is the biochemical breakdown of glucose yielding ATP.
  *Reason: Mixing physical gas exchange with biochemical phosphorylation causes mark loss.*
