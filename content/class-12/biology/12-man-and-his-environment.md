### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

  ✓ **Correction:** Pyramid of energy is ALWAYS upright because energy is lost as heat at each trophic transfer (10% law).
  *Reason: Thermodynamics dictates irreversible energy loss across trophic levels.*
