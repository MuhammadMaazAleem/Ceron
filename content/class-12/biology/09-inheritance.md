### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing phenotype with genotype in monohybrid crosses.
  ✓ **Correction:** Genotype is the genetic allele combination (e.g. 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt); phenotype is observable physical appearance (3 Tall : 1 Dwarf).
  *Reason: Board examiners deduct marks if ratio types are mixed.*
