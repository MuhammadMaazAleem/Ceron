### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing ectoderm, mesoderm, and endoderm organ derivations.
  ✓ **Correction:** Ectoderm forms skin and nervous system; mesoderm forms muscle, bone, and heart; endoderm forms gut and lungs.
  *Reason: Triploblastic tissue lineage is heavily tested in Section B.*
