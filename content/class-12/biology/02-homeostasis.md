### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Stating that ADH decreases urine concentration.
  ✓ **Correction:** ADH increases water reabsorption in collecting ducts, making urine more concentrated and hypertonic.
  *Reason: ADH conserves body water during dehydration.*
