### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing PCR stages: denaturation, annealing, and extension.
  ✓ **Correction:** Denaturation occurs at 94°C (strands separate); Annealing at 54°C (primers bind); Extension at 72°C (Taq polymerase synthesizes new DNA).
  *Reason: Temperature sequencing is required for full 4 marks.*
