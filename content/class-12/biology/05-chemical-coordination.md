### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing endocrine glands with exocrine glands.
  ✓ **Correction:** Endocrine glands are ductless and secrete hormones directly into the bloodstream; exocrine glands secrete via ducts.
  *Reason: Ductless delivery enables widespread systemic hormonal communication.*
