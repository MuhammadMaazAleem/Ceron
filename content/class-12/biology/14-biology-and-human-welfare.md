### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing somatic gene therapy with germline gene therapy.
  ✓ **Correction:** Somatic gene therapy alters body cells and is not inheritable; germline gene therapy modifies gametes and is passed to future generations.
  *Reason: Heritability distinction is an essential examiner test point.*
