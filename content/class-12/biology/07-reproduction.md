### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Stating that oogenesis produces four functional ova.
  ✓ **Correction:** Oogenesis produces only one functional ovum and three non-functional polar bodies.
  *Reason: Unequal cytokinesis conserves cytoplasm and nutrient reserves for the developing embryo.*
