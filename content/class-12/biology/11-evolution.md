### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing homologous organs with analogous organs.
  ✓ **Correction:** Homologous structures share common embryonic origin and anatomy (human arm, bat wing); analogous organs share function but differ in origin (bird wing, insect wing).
  *Reason: Divergent vs convergent evolution depends on structural origin.*
