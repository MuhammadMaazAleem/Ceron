### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Pascal’s Principle** (پاسکل کا قانون): Pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.
- **Young’s Modulus** (ینگز ماڈیولس): Ratio of tensile stress to tensile strain: Y = (F·L) / (A·ΔL).

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Hydraulic Press Force Multiplication
**Problem:** In a hydraulic press, an input force of 100 N is applied on a piston of area 0.02 m². Find the output force on a large piston of area 0.8 m².
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Pascal Law:** P1 = P2 => F1 / A1 = F2 / A2.
- **Step 2: Solve for F2:** F2 = F1 × (A2 / A1) = 100 × (0.8 / 0.02) = 100 × 40 = 4000 N.
**Answer:** `Output Force F2 = 4000 Newtons`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing stress (Force / Area, Pa) with strain (fractional change, dimensionless).
  ✓ **Correction:** Stress has units of N/m² (Pascal); strain is a pure ratio with NO units.
  *Reason: Units verification is rigorously checked in board marking.*
