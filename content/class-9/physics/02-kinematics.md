### 3. Deep Conceptual Explanation
Kinematics deals with the motion of bodies without reference to force. Concepts of distance vs displacement, speed vs velocity, and uniform acceleration. Derivation and practical application of the three standard equations of motion under constant acceleration and gravity free-fall.

> **خلاصہ (Urdu Summary):**
> قوت کا ذکر کیے بغیر اجسام کی حرکت کا مطالعہ۔ فاصلہ اور ہٹاؤ، رفتار اور ویلاسٹی، یکساں ایکسلریشن، اور حرکت کی تین بنیادی مساواتوں کا حسابی اطلاق۔

### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Displacement** (ہٹاؤ): Shortest directed distance between initial and final points (vector quantity).
- **Acceleration** (اسراع (ایکسلریشن)): Rate of change of velocity with respect to time: a = (vf - vi) / t.

```
First Equation of Motion: vf = vi + at
Relates initial and final velocity with uniform acceleration and time.
```
```
Second Equation of Motion: S = vi·t + 1/2 a·t²
Calculates distance covered under constant acceleration.
```
```
Third Equation of Motion: 2aS = vf² - vi²
Time-independent kinematic relation.
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Car Braking Distance Calculation
**Problem:** A car moves with a velocity of 20 m/s. The brakes are applied and it comes to rest in 5 seconds. Find the retardation and distance covered.
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Given Data:** vi = 20 m/s, vf = 0 m/s, t = 5 s.
- **Step 2: Calculate Retardation (a):** a = (vf - vi) / t = (0 - 20) / 5 = -4 m/s².
- **Step 3: Calculate Distance (S):** S = vi·t + 1/2 a·t² = 20(5) + 0.5(-4)(25) = 100 - 50 = 50 m.
**Answer:** `Retardation = -4 m/s², Total Distance = 50 meters`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Using speed instead of velocity when direction changes.
  ✓ **Correction:** Remember velocity is a vector; direction must be accounted for.
  *Reason: Displacement can be zero while total path distance is non-zero.*
