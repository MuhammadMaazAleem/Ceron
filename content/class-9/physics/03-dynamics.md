### 3. Deep Conceptual Explanation
Study of motion considering the forces producing it. Newton’s three laws of motion (Inertia, F = ma, Action and Reaction), momentum p = mv, law of conservation of momentum, centripetal force Fc = mv²/r, and the nature of friction (static vs kinetic, rolling friction).

### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Inertia** (جمود (انرشیا)): Property of a body to resist any change in its state of rest or uniform motion.
- **Momentum** (مومینٹم): Quantity of motion possessed by a body: p = mv (kg·m/s).

```
Newton’s Second Law: F = m · a
Net force produces acceleration in the direction of force.
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Force Required to Accelerate a Mass
**Problem:** A 1200 kg vehicle accelerates from rest to 15 m/s in 6 seconds. Calculate the net force acting on it.
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Calculate acceleration:** a = (15 - 0) / 6 = 2.5 m/s².
- **Step 2: Apply F = ma:** F = 1200 kg × 2.5 m/s² = 3000 N.
**Answer:** `Force = 3000 Newtons`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Confusing mass (scalar, kg) with weight (force vector, N = mg).
  ✓ **Correction:** Weight changes with gravitational field g; mass is an intrinsic constant.
  *Reason: Examiners deduct marks if Newtons are written for mass or kg for weight.*
