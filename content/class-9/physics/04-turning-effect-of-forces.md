### 3. Deep Conceptual Explanation
Rotational mechanics: torque (moment of force) τ = r × F, principle of moments, center of mass and center of gravity, couple, and the two conditions of equilibrium: ΣF = 0 (translational equilibrium) and Στ = 0 (rotational equilibrium). Stable, unstable, and neutral equilibrium states.

> **خلاصہ (Urdu Summary):**
> گردشی حرکیات: ٹارک یا قوت کا گھماؤ، مومنٹس کا اصول، سنٹر آف گریویٹی، اور توازن کی دو شرائط۔

### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Torque** (ٹارک (گردشی اثر)): The turning effect of a force about an axis: τ = Force × Moment Arm.
- **Equilibrium** (توازن (ایکوی لبریم)): State when no net force and no net torque act on a body.

```
Torque Formula: τ = F · d
d is the perpendicular distance from pivot to line of action.
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Spanner Torque Calculation
**Problem:** A mechanic applies a 50 N force perpendicular to a spanner of length 0.25 m to loosen a nut. Find the torque applied.
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Formula:** τ = F × d.
- **Step 2: Calculation:** τ = 50 N × 0.25 m = 12.5 N·m.
**Answer:** `Torque = 12.5 N·m`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Measuring moment arm along the force vector instead of perpendicular to it.
  ✓ **Correction:** Always ensure the moment arm distance d is strictly 90° to the force line.
  *Reason: Parallel forces create zero torque.*
