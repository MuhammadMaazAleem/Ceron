### 3. Deep Conceptual Explanation
Newton’s universal law of gravitation F = G m1 m2 / r², determining the mass of Earth (Me ≈ 6.0 × 10²⁴ kg), variation of gravitational acceleration g with altitude, artificial satellites, and geostationary orbits.

> **خلاصہ (Urdu Summary):**
> نیوٹن کا قانونِ کششِ ثقل، زمین کا ماس معلوم کرنا، بلندی کے ساتھ کشش ثقل میں تبدیلی اور مصنوعی سیاروں کی مداری رفتار۔

### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Universal Gravitational Constant G** (کشش ثقل کا مستقل (G)): Value G = 6.673 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² throughout the universe.
- **Orbital Velocity** (مداری رفتار): Velocity required to keep a satellite in circular orbit around Earth: v = √(gh).

```
Newton’s Law of Gravitation: F = G · (m₁ · m₂) / r²
Gravitational attraction between two masses.
```
```
Mass of Earth: M_e = (g · R_e²) / G ≈ 6.0 × 10²⁴ kg
Calculated using surface gravity g = 9.8 m/s².
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Weight of a Body on the Moon
**Problem:** A body has a mass of 60 kg on Earth. If surface gravity on the moon is 1.62 m/s², what is its mass and weight on the moon?
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Mass is Constant:** Mass remains 60 kg on Earth, Moon, or in deep space.
- **Step 2: Moon Weight:** W_moon = m × g_moon = 60 kg × 1.62 m/s² = 97.2 N.
**Answer:** `Mass = 60 kg, Weight on Moon = 97.2 N`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Writing that mass changes on different planets.
  ✓ **Correction:** Mass is the amount of matter (constant); only weight (gravitational pull) changes.
  *Reason: A common trick in BISE Swat MCQs.*
