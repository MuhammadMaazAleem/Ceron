### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Specific Heat Capacity** (مخصوص حرارت): Amount of heat required to raise temperature of 1 kg mass by 1 K: c = Q / (mΔT).
- **Latent Heat** (مخفی حرارت): Heat absorbed or released during phase change without changing temperature.

```
Specific Heat Equation: Q = m · c · ΔT
Heat energy absorbed or released.
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Heat Needed to Warm Water
**Problem:** Calculate the heat required to raise the temperature of 2 kg of water from 20°C to 70°C (c = 4200 J/kg·K).
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: ΔT:** ΔT = 70 - 20 = 50 K.
- **Step 2: Q = mcΔT:** Q = 2 kg × 4200 J/kg·K × 50 K = 420,000 J = 420 kJ.
**Answer:** `Heat required = 420 kJ`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Thinking temperature changes during melting or boiling.
  ✓ **Correction:** During phase change, temperature remains constant as latent heat breaks intermolecular bonds.
  *Reason: Fundamental concept of latent heat.*
