### 3. Deep Conceptual Explanation
Work W = F · d cos θ, kinetic energy KE = 1/2 mv², potential energy PE = mgh, interconversion of kinetic and potential energy, law of conservation of energy, power P = W/t (Watts), and efficiency = (Useful Output / Total Input) × 100%.

### 4. Key Terms, Definitions & Formulas
- **Joule** (جول (Joule)): Work done when a force of 1 N moves an object through 1 m in its direction.
- **Power** (پاور): Rate of doing work: P = W / t (measured in Watts: 1 W = 1 J/s).

```
Kinetic Energy: KE = 1/2 · m · v²
Energy possessed by virtue of motion.
```
```
Gravitational Potential Energy: PE = m · g · h
Energy possessed by virtue of position.
```

### 6. Worked Examples

#### Example 1: Work Done by a Crane
**Problem:** A crane lifts a 500 kg load to a vertical height of 20 meters in 10 seconds. Find work done and power developed (g = 10 m/s²).
**Step-by-Step Solution:**
- **Step 1: Work Done:** W = F × h = mgh = 500 × 10 × 20 = 100,000 J (100 kJ).
- **Step 2: Power:** P = W / t = 100,000 / 10 = 10,000 Watts (10 kW).
**Answer:** `Work = 100,000 Joules, Power = 10 kW`


### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Assuming work is done when carrying a load horizontally at constant height.
  ✓ **Correction:** When force is upward and motion is horizontal, angle θ = 90°, cos 90° = 0, so work done by gravity is zero.
  *Reason: Definition of scientific work requires displacement in the direction of force.*
