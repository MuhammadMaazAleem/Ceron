### 7. Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

- ❌ **Mistake:** Forgetting ± sign before square root in quadratic formula.
  ✓ **Correction:** Always include ±: x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / (2a).
  *Reason: Omitting ± discards the second valid root of the equation.*
